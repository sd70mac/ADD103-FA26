Lead Units

BNSF 3989 leads a southbound mixed train south of Savanna, Illinois. Lead units are placed on the head-end of a train, and are where it is normally controlled from.

Updated September 2026

Distributed Power Units

BNSF 8111 brings up the rear of a southbound mixed train. Distributed Power Units are placed in the middle and/or on the rear of a train to provide additional horsepower, braking force, and to both maintain and adjust train-line air brake pressure. A locomotive on the rear can also be swapped to be the lead unit to facilitate changes in direction at terminals.
