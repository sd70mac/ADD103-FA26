The Future of Air Travel

A 5 Minute Talk

  • Drones
    1. package delivery
    2. first aid items
  • Helicopters
    1. Hybrid electric
    2. Drone-like designs with many propellers
  • Balloons
    1. Weather
    2. Research
    3. Broadband, Ties in with satellites
    4. Aerial electricity generation
  • Hybrid Jets
    1. Traction motors in wheels
      • Reduce emissions when ground taxiing
      • Improve acceleration and braking on runways
    2. Battery power units
      • engines can be switched off when on the ground
  • Supersonic jets
    1. Hydrogen
    2. Cellulosic biofuels
    3. Synthetic fuels
  • Rockets
    1. Much shorter trip times over very long distances
