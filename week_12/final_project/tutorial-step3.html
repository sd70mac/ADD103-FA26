Step Three

You can either just select the type of address, either short or long, for the locomotive or railcar decoder, and some other basic settings directly, including if the locomotive will run on analog direct current or not.  You can save or discard your entry here, or open the comprehensive programmer to change more settings beyond the address and roster ID.  If changing the decoder address or turning analog DC mode on or off is all that you wanted to do, congratulations, you are done.  If you wish to do more, continue to step four.
